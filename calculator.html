Protein Priority

Aim for 1.8g to 2.2g of protein per kg of lean mass to protect and rebuild muscle tissue during caloric deficits or heavy training.

Glycogen Fueling

Complex carbohydrates fuel high-intensity glycolytic work like sprints and heavy compounds. Time carbs around your training window.

Hormonal Health

Dietary fats are crucial for testosterone, estrogen, and joint health. Keep healthy fats at 20-30% of total daily energy intake.
