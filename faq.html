FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

  • FAQ
Are there any lock-in contracts or cancellation penalties?
No. At FITORA, we believe in earning your loyalty every single month. All our memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract lock-ins. You can cancel, downgrade, or pause your membership anytime through the FITORA Member App with 7 days notice prior to your next billing cycle.
Can I freeze my membership if I am traveling or injured?
Yes! You can place your membership on medical or travel freeze for up to 90 consecutive days per calendar year at zero charge. Simply submit your freeze request via the mobile app or talk to our front desk concierge.
How do studio class reservations work?
Premium and Elite VIP members can reserve spots in any studio class up to 7 days in advance. To preserve optimal coaching ratios, classes are capped at 16 to 20 athletes. If a class fills up, you can join the automated waitlist, which notifies you via SMS if a slot opens.
Are personal trainers included with my membership?
Every new member receives a complimentary InBody biometric body composition assessment and 1-on-1 strategy orientation. Premium members receive 1 monthly coaching session, while Elite VIP members receive 4 monthly sessions. Additional personal training packs can be added anytime.
What amenities are provided in the locker suites?
Our locker suites feature digital keypad lockers, rainhead showers, complimentary plush organic towels, Malin+Goetz apothecary toiletries, Dyson Supersonic hair dryers, filtered cold water refill stations, and private changing cabanas.
How does the Cold Plunge & Recovery Spa work?
Our recovery wing includes commercial Morozko Forge contrast cold tubs maintained at 4°C (39°F) alongside commercial dry Cedar Saunas and eucalyptus steam rooms. Premium and Elite members have access following their training sessions. Clean swimwear is required.
What is the shipping policy for the FITORA Pro Shop?
We offer fast 2-3 business day domestic shipping across the US. Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping. You can also select "Pay at Front Desk Pickup" during checkout to pick up apparel and supplements at our club counter immediately.
